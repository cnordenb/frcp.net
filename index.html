About Fresh Random Civ Picker

Fresh Random Civ Picker, web version

Created by Carl Nordenbrink, alias Hjörleif. Also available as a desktop app.

Custom civ pool, draw log and user settings are saved in this browser only. Share links carry a copy of the custom civ pool, and optionally of this round's draws.
